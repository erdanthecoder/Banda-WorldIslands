World Islands for Windows

The island game as an app on your computer. Free.

⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · about 110 MB · no admin rights needed

How to install

  1. Open the downloaded file WorldIslands-Setup.exe.
  2. Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. This appears for new apps that are not yet signed with a paid certificate. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  3. The app installs for your user only and opens. You will find “World Islands” on your desktop and in the Start menu.
  4. Sign in with your FlexiHub account, the same as on the website.

Why it is safe

  • It only opens World Islands and its sign-in pages. Any other link opens in your normal browser.
  • The game page cannot touch your files or your computer (sandboxed, no Node.js).
  • Only secure connections (HTTPS). Fake or broken certificates are always refused.
  • Only World Islands may use the camera and microphone, and only when you start a call or the Mood camera. No other permissions.
  • Installs without administrator rights. Uninstall any time from Windows Settings → Apps.
  • The game updates itself — every new World Islands update is in the app right away.

Check the file (optional)

In PowerShell run: Get-FileHash WorldIslands-Setup.exe — it must match the SHA-256 shown above.
