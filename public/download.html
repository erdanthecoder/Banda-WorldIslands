World Islands apps 2.0

Play World Islands as an app on your computer or phone. Free, safe, and it updates by itself.

💻 Windows

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · about 110 MB · no admin rights needed

⬇ Download for Windows

  1. Open the downloaded file WorldIslands-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info”, then “Run anyway” (new apps without a paid certificate show this).
  3. World Islands installs and opens. It is on your desktop and in the Start menu.

🤖 Android

Android 7 or newer · phones and tablets · needs Google Chrome

⬇ Download for Android

  1. Open the downloaded file WorldIslands.apk.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to “Install unknown apps” (Settings), then tap Install.
  3. Tap World Islands on your home screen. It opens full screen.

Safe by design

  • The apps only open World Islands and its sign-in pages. Other links open in your normal browser.
  • The game can't touch your files. Only secure connections (HTTPS).
  • Camera and microphone are only used when you start a call or the Mood camera — never recorded or sent as video.
  • Every World Islands update is in the apps right away — no need to download again.
